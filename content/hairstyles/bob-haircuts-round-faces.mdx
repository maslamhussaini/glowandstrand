If you've been told bobs don't work on round faces, whoever said that was wrong. The issue isn't the cut — it's the *length and angle*. The right bob on a round face adds the illusion of length and creates jaw definition that genuinely changes how your face reads.

I have a round face. I've tested most of these. Here's what actually works.

## What to look for in a bob for round faces

Before the list — two rules. First, **avoid one-length blunt bobs that end at chin level**. That's the exact point where your face is widest, and cutting there widens it further. Second, **asymmetry and angles are your best friend**. An angled bob, a lob, or a bob with layers all draw the eye vertically instead of horizontally.

## The 27 looks

### 1. The angled A-line bob

Shorter at the back, longer toward the front — this is the most flattering bob shape for round faces, full stop. The forward angle creates a visual jawline even if your natural jaw is soft. Ask your stylist for a 2-3 inch graduation from back to front.

**Why it works:** The long front pieces frame the face vertically, drawing the eye downward and creating perceived length.

### 2. The chin-length lob (long bob)

The lob — anything between chin and collarbone — is the most versatile option. It sits below the widest point of a round face, which automatically creates a slimming effect.

**Why it works:** Length past the chin creates a vertical line that visually elongates your face shape.

---

### 3. Layered bob with curtain bangs

Layers add movement and reduce bulk, which is exactly what round faces need. Pair with soft curtain bangs that part in the middle — they create a vertical centre line that adds length.

**Why it works:** Curtain bangs + layers = vertical emphasis on both the top and sides of your face.

👉 [A wide-tooth comb](https://www.amazon.com) is essential for defining layers without frizz on fine hair.

---

### 4. Asymmetric bob

One side longer than the other creates instant visual interest and breaks the symmetry of a round face. This works especially well if you naturally part your hair to one side.

**Why it works:** Asymmetry draws attention away from the roundness and creates angles where there aren't any naturally.

---

## Final thoughts

The best bob for a round face is one that ends below your chin, has some angle or asymmetry, and avoids blunt horizontal cuts at jaw level. Everything else is personal preference.
